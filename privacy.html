Privacy policy of home-server-backup

home-server-backup is a private Google login app. Its owner uses it to store encrypted backups of one home server in the owner's own Google Drive. Nobody else uses it.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the app, with the owner's own Google account. The app accepts no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Google Drive, scope drive.file: only the files that the app created itself in the owner's Drive.
  • The app does not access any other Google data: no other Drive files, no email, no contacts, no calendar, no profile data.

How the app uses the data

  • The backup program on the owner's server creates, reads, checks and deletes its own backup files.
  • Every backup is encrypted on the server before the upload. Google stores only encrypted files.
  • The Google login token stays on the owner's server. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.

Sharing

The app shares no data with anyone. It sells no data, shows no ads and uses no analytics. These pages use no cookies and no tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs by home-server-backup adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove the app's access

In the Google account, open Third-party apps and services and remove home-server-backup. Files that the app created stay in the Drive until the owner deletes them.

Contact

Open an issue in the home-server-backup repository on GitHub.
